## Evaluation data (downloaded, not committed)

| Dataset | Licence | Use |
|---|---|---|
| [vocadito](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5578807) (Bittner, Pasalo, Bosch, Meseguer Brocal and Rubinstein, 2021) | CC BY 4.0 | Scoring pitch, onset and key detection. Fetched by `tools/python/fetch_vocadito.py` into `testdata/external/`. |
| [Children's Song Dataset](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4916302) (Choi, Kim, Park, Yong and Nam, 2020) | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 | Scoring key detection on real singing. Fetched by `tools/python/fetch_csd.py` into `testdata/external/`. Non-commercial, so it is used only for evaluation, never built into the app or committed. |
| Bach chorales and Ryan's Mammoth Collection (1883), from the music21 corpus | The music is in the public domain; the encodings come with music21 | Melodies for the synthetic key test set, sung by `tools/python/make_key_testset.py` into `testdata/external/keyset/` (and a held-out set of other tunes into `keyset-holdout/`). Generated locally, not committed. |

## Sound assets

| Asset | Licence | Notes |
|-------|---------|-------|
| `assets/sfz/test/sine.sfz` | AGPLv3 (part of this repo) | Uses sfizz's built-in sine generator; no samples. |
| `assets/sfz/afro-kit/kick/*.wav` | AGPLv3 (part of this repo) | The Afrobeats kick, synthesized by `tools/python/make_afro_kit.py`. |
| `assets/sfz/afro-kit/afro-kit.sfz`, `samples.json` | AGPLv3 (part of this repo) | The kit mapping, generated by `tools/python/make_afro_kit.py`. |
| [Versilian Community Sample Library](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) (VCSL), 116 percussion and drum samples | CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) | Snare, hi-hat, cymbal, toms, claps, congas, bongos, shaker, cabasa, tambourine, agogo, cowbell, claves and woodblock, from commit `dfcf4a49` of the `sfz` branch. Downloaded by `tools/python/fetch_assets.py` into `assets/sfz/afro-kit/vcsl/`, checked against SHA-256 sums, and not committed. CI bundles them with the Windows build. |
| `assets/grooves/afrobeats/*` | AGPLv3 (part of this repo) | The Afrobeats patterns (drums, percussion, bass, piano and pad, with intense variations), generated by `tools/python/make_grooves.py`. |
| `assets/grooves/worship-6-8/*` | AGPLv3 (part of this repo) | The worship style in 6/8, generated by `tools/python/make_grooves.py`. |
| `assets/sfz/bass/synth-bass.sfz`, `assets/sfz/pad/warm-pad.sfz` | AGPLv3 (part of this repo) | The synth bass and pad, made by sfizz's built-in oscillators; no samples. Generated by `tools/python/make_band_kits.py`. |
| `assets/sfz/keys/piano.sfz`, `samples.json` | AGPLv3 (part of this repo) | The piano mapping, with per-sample levels and tuning, generated by `tools/python/make_band_kits.py`. |
| VCSL Kawai grand piano, 11 samples (third velocity layer, A2 to E6) | CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) | From `Chordophones/Zithers/Grand Piano, Kawai` at the same VCSL commit. Downloaded by `tools/python/fetch_assets.py` into `assets/sfz/keys/vcsl/`, checked against SHA-256 sums, and not committed. CI bundles them with the Windows build. |

Further kits should also be downloaded by the fetch script rather than committed.
Prefer CC0 or CC-BY kits and avoid non-commercial licences.
